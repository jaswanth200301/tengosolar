Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about subsidies, DISCOM feasibility, and equipment safety.

How does the ₹78,000 PM Surya Ghar subsidy get credited?
Under the central scheme, residential rooftop installations up to 2 kW receive ₹60,000. An additional 1 kW adds ₹18,000, maxing out at ₹78,000 for 3 kW and higher systems. Following APCPDCL inspection and commissioning, the subsidy is disbursed directly to your Aadhaar-linked bank account within 30 days.
Does expressing interest commit me to any payment?
Not at all. Submitting an expression of interest only registers your terrace in our feasibility queue. You only make decisions after reviewing the engineering assessment and formal contract.
What is APCPDCL bidirectional net-metering?
A net meter spins forward when you pull electricity from the grid at night, and spins backward during the afternoon as your solar panels export surplus energy. Your bimonthly bill charges only for the net consumed units.
Will drilling anchor bolts cause water leakage during monsoon rains?
No. Our technicians use marine-grade stainless steel anchor bolts sealed with specialized epoxy and weatherproof silicone grouting to protect RCC terrace slabs from coastal rains and cyclone winds.
How much shadow-free rooftop space do I need for a 3 kW plant?
A standard 3.3 kW array consists of six 550W Tier-1 bifacial panels, requiring roughly 250 to 300 square feet of unobstructed South-facing rooftop area.
