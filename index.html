Frequently asked questions

Still unsure? Here's what people usually ask before expressing interest.

Does expressing interest cost anything?
No. It's a free enquiry — you're only sharing your details so we can get back to you with real numbers.
Am I committing to buy anything?
Not at all. It simply starts a conversation. You decide whether to move forward after you see a quote.
How soon will someone contact me?
Within 24 hours, usually sooner. We'll call or message using the phone number you provide.
Is my roof even suitable for solar?
Most roofs are. Share your rough roof size and shading situation in the message field, and we'll confirm during the follow-up call.
What if I change my mind later?
Completely fine — there's no obligation at any stage before you sign an actual installation agreement.

Got a different question?

Leave it in the message field when you express interest and we'll answer directly.
