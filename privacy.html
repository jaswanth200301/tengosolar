Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Clashers Union by Rowdy is owned and run by Rowdy. Questions about this policy: contact@clashersunion.tengosolar.com.

What we collect

Visitors: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views and basic technical details such as country and browser. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites. Our hosts (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may also process your IP address to deliver pages and keep the site secure.

Accounts: if you create an account we collect your player name, player tag, current clan name and tag (optional), email address and a password. Passwords are stored in a protected form that we cannot read. To confirm a tag is yours, you give us a one-time token from the game, which we send to Supercell's official game API and do not store. We keep only whether your tag is verified.

Login and bot checks: your browser keeps a login session so you stay signed in. The signup, login and password reset forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated abuse.

Chat: messages you post, with your player name and tag, can be seen by the other members of that chat (everyone verified for the global chat, or the members of a clan chat). You can delete your own messages, report others and block people. If you delete your account, your name is removed from your old messages and they show as "Former member". Moderators can see reports and remove messages.

Public player profiles: anyone can look up a player tag. We show only public in-game information fetched from Supercell's official game API, and we keep a copy for a few minutes. We never show your email or other account details on it.

How we use it

To run your account, show public player profiles, keep the community safe, contact you about your account and understand how many people visit. We do not sell your information.

Who helps us run the site

Service providers process data for us only to provide these services: Supabase (database and login), Resend (sending emails), Cloudflare (domain, bot checks and analytics) and GitHub (hosting). Some of them may process data outside India.

Age

You must be at least 13 to use Clashers Union. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before signing up. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think we have, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can delete your account yourself from your account page. You can also email us to see, correct or delete your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
