Solar for Every Situation

Choose your category below to see if solar is right for you.

🏠 Residential Rooftop

For homeowners with ₹1,500-5,000 monthly bills

Install panels on your roof. Use power directly. Zero DISCOM charges on your solar generation.

✓ Why viable: Subsidy reduces cost by 40-50%. Tariffs ₹5.5-7.5/unit = 4-6 year payback

🏢 Commercial Rooftop

For offices, shops, hospitals with ₹10k-50k bills

Rooftop solar for your commercial property. No DISCOM charges. Direct consumption advantage.

✓ Why viable: Higher tariffs (₹8-12/unit) = 2.5-3 year payback. Tax depreciation benefits available

⚙️ Commercial Open Access

For large businesses with ₹50k+ monthly bills

Solar farm delivers power via grid. Fixed tariff locks in rates. Protects against tariff increases.

✓ Why viable: Fixed ₹11/unit tariff vs volatile grid rates. Huge consumption = faster payback

🏭 Industrial Rooftop

For factories, warehouses with ₹1,00k+ bills

Factory rooftop solar. Direct consumption. 40% tax depreciation benefit in Year 1.

✓ Why viable: High tariffs + tax benefits = 18-22% IRR. Accelerated payback despite large investment

🔗 Industrial Open Access

For heavy industry with ₹1,00k+ consumption

Large solar farm. Grid-delivered power. Fixed ₹9.5/unit tariff. Long-term certainty.

✓ Why viable: Lower DISCOM charges than commercial. Huge scale = lowest per-unit cost. 2.5-3 year payback

👥 Community Power Plant

For villages, societies, communities

Shared solar system. Cost split among households. Benefit distributed equally.

✓ Why viable: Shared investment = lower per-home cost. No individual burden. Democracy in sustainability

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025  ·  Tengo Solar, India

On this page:

1. What we collect 2. How we use it 3. How we store it 4. Who we share it with 5. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023) 6. Cookies 7. Children's privacy 8. Contact us

Tengo Solar ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and applicable Indian law. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and your rights as a data principal.

1. What we collect

We collect data that you provide voluntarily when using our calculator or submitting a consultation request:

  • Identity data: your name
  • Contact data: phone number, email address
  • Location data: your state (for tariff accuracy)
  • Usage data: calculator inputs (consumption, bill amount, system type), how you heard about us
  • Account data: if you create an account — email address and encrypted password (via Firebase Authentication)
  • Technical data: browser type, device type, IP address, pages visited (if analytics is enabled)

We do not collect sensitive personal data such as financial account numbers, Aadhaar numbers, health data, or biometric data.

2. How we use it

  • To contact you about your solar consultation request
  • To send you a confirmation email when you submit a form
  • To save your calculations in your personal dashboard (if logged in)
  • To improve the accuracy of our calculator for your state
  • To understand which channels bring users to our site (aggregated, anonymous)

We will never use your data to send unsolicited marketing, sell to third-party installer databases, or build advertising profiles.

3. How we store it

Form submission data is processed by Web3Forms (web3forms.com) and delivered to our business email. Web3Forms does not store your data beyond the transmission window. Account and calculation data is stored in Google Firebase / Firestore, hosted on Google Cloud servers with servers in the Asia region. Firebase uses AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS in transit.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to fulfil your consultation request, and for a maximum of 3 years after your last interaction, after which it is deleted.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with:

  • Google Firebase — account and calculation storage
  • Web3Forms — form submission delivery
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics (if enabled; you can opt out via our cookie banner)
  • Meta Pixel — advertising analytics (if enabled; you can opt out via our cookie banner)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

5. Your rights under the DPDP Act 2023

As a data principal under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") of your data
  • Grievance redressal — raise a complaint with our Data Protection Officer
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights on your behalf

To exercise any right, email us at our contact form. We will respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies

We use the following cookies:

  • Essential cookies: required for the site to function (auth session, preferences). Cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics — used to understand aggregate traffic. Enabled only with your consent.
  • Marketing cookies: Meta Pixel — used for measuring ad effectiveness. Enabled only with your consent.

You can withdraw consent at any time by clearing your browser cookies. Our cookie banner will reappear on your next visit.

7. Children's privacy

Our services are not directed at persons under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

8. Contact & grievances

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may raise a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India once constituted under the DPDP Act 2023.
